01 · The Business Problem

Can wine data tell us what comes next?

Wine is not only an art — it is also a business of precision, timing and taste. The central challenge is to understand what has sold, where demand is concentrated, how performance changes over time, and which wine categories may lead the next production cycle.

Questions the project answers

  • Which wines and varieties perform best?
  • Which vendors and regions contribute most?
  • How has sales performance changed through time?
  • How does price relate to sales performance?
  • What can historical patterns tell us about future production?

04 · Pricing Intelligence

Does a higher price mean higher performance?

Interpret carefully: the original project observed strong sales among higher-priced wines in its sample. That is an association, not evidence that price alone causes sales.

Conclusion

The Vivino project demonstrates a complete analytics journey: collect → clean → explore → visualize → model → forecast → communicate. With a documented forecasting pipeline and real underlying data, the same framework can become a reusable wine-market intelligence product.
